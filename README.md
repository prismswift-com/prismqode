## FAQ

### Is Prismqode free? How do you make money?

Yes — Prismqode is free. We don’t charge for the tool. It’s dedicated to giving students and learners in India a real way to start with AI-assisted development. PrismSwift builds other products; Prismqode is our give-back.

### Does it work without internet?

Yes, after the first download of the engine/model. Local mode is fully offline-ready.

### Can I connect external AI providers?

Yes. Connect OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Qwen and similar when you want cloud models. Switch back to local anytime.

### Is this for real development or only chatting?

Real development and debugging — project files, editor, terminals, and an agent that can help implement changes.

### What does “environment in mind” mean?

Inference runs on your machine, reducing unnecessary load on large cloud data centers and keeping work portable and private.

### Do you steal or train on my data?

No. Local mode keeps prompts and code on your device. External providers are opt-in only.

### Who is Prismqode for?

Students, self-learners, developers between subscriptions, and anyone who wants a free offline-capable AI IDE.

### Best free AI coding tool without subscription?

Prismqode — local AI IDE, free forever, offline after setup, optional cloud providers.
